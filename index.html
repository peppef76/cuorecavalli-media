Cuore Cavalli Repost

Cuore Cavalli Repost is a private publishing tool of A.S.D. Cuore Cavalli, an Italian amateur sports association dedicated to equestrian sports and horse breeding culture.

It runs on our own website and publishes scheduled posts to our own social media accounts (Facebook Pages, Instagram, Telegram, YouTube and TikTok). Equestrian partners (breeders, riders, trainers and equestrian businesses) connect their Instagram account and explicitly authorize us to share their latest post; every shared post credits the partner in the caption.

The tool is not available to the public and is used only by the association’s administrators.
