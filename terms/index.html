Cuore Cavalli Repost is an internal tool of A.S.D. Cuore Cavalli. It is used only by the association’s administrators to publish posts to the association’s own social media accounts.

Partners

By connecting their Instagram account, partners authorize A.S.D. Cuore Cavalli to share their latest public post on the association’s accounts, credited to them. Partners confirm they own the rights to the content they share and can revoke the authorization at any time.

Platforms

Content is published in compliance with the terms of each platform (Meta, TikTok, YouTube, Telegram). Content that violates a platform’s rules is not published.
